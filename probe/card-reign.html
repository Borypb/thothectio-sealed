Medivh the Prophet

Master Hero — 1/30 Human Prophet — Play Cost: 5

8 → Search your deck and/or hand for a master hero that does not have the same name as a card you control. Put that hero into play as an ally. (It is no longer a hero.)
The allies Medivh puts into play retain their ATK, and all powers.

If one of those allies leaves play and then returns, or if a copy of one is put into play, it enters as a Master Hero, replacing Medivh. If multiple Master Heroes enter play under your control simultaneously, you choose the order in which they replace your hero.

"At long last, I have returned to set things right."

Art by: Alex Garner
